# BIP-XR-Project: XR Smart House

A VR digital twin of the smart-home lab at TalTech Virumaa College. Put on a headset, walk through a virtual copy of the house, press a lamp, and the real lamp switches. Every device in VR is linked to a Home Assistant entity, so a change on either side shows up on the other within about 3 seconds.

Built in Unity as a BIP project.

## What you can do

- Switch lights on and off, and dim the living-room light with a virtual wall dimmer
- Open and close curtains
- Switch smart plugs and read their power draw
- Unlock the front door and lock or unlock the Z-Wave lock
- Set the thermostat's heating mode and temperature
- See changes made in the real room (by hand or in the Home Assistant app) appear in VR
